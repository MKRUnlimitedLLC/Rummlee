Decision rights02

The company

People already sell to neighbors. They should not have to publish a house to do it.

Facebook and Craigslist are free because the seller pays in time, no-shows, and a stranger who knows the street. eBay works when the thing should travel. A lamp, a mixer, a bin of clothes, a bike should not travel. They need a counter, and money that is already held before anyone drives.

Pay first

Asking price, or one offer. Each side gets one counteroffer. A decline ends it. The item is held. A second person cannot buy it.

Meet second

Official store first. Public place if the store is wrong. In person only if the seller turns it on. The address appears after payment, not on the card.

Nothing ships

Rummlee is the handoff, not a carrier. Over 50 pounds, or not in an outer box, stays in person. A couch does not go to a store shelf.

What it is03

Who it is for

We speak first to women in cities and suburbs. The doors are open to everyone.

The person we write for will not put a house number in a post, will not wait on a porch, and will pay a small fee to avoid both. She is why privacy is the product and not a setting.

The listing, the offer, and the counter do not ask whether the member is a woman. Handles, not legal names. A man uses the same handoff. Copy that says “for women only” is a design miss. Copy that ignores why she would rather not use Marketplace is also a miss. That line is yours to draw.

The operating loop05

The counter

Two codes. The long one is the person. The short one is the box.

Seller

Writes the short code on the box. Shows the phone code only when the associate is ready to scan. The phone code hides again. A photo of that screen is as good as handing over the phone, so it does not go on the label.

Store

Scans the phone, then matches the short code. Puts the box away. Two days for the seller to drop off, or the buyer is refunded. Five days after scan-in, or the buyer is refunded. Several boxes from one seller share the phone scan and keep their own short codes. Each box is confirmed by itself.

Buyer

Scans their phone. The screen lists the codes to pull. They type the code on that box. Typing a code by hand is the backup if the camera is broken. Scanning is the normal way. No name, no price, and no address on that screen.

In person uses the same idea: both confirm with a scan or a 6-character code. There is an “I’m on my way” button. It is not a map of the house. Showing up on time becomes part of reputation. The seller is paid about 48 hours after both confirm, not at the curb.

Chain of custody06

The price

The seller names two numbers. The buyer sees one.

Asking

Public. The buyer can pay it and the item is held. Pay-asking stays available while the listing is live. There is no second, higher “buy it now” stacked on top.

Lowest

Hidden. The buyer can offer under asking. The seller has one counteroffer, between the lowest and the asking. The buyer has one reply. A decline by either side ends it. +++ can reveal that hidden number five times a month. That is a membership trick, not a public price.

This is the whole negotiation. It is short on purpose. A weekend of messages is what people already have, and they hate it. If someone wants a longer bargain, they are in the wrong product. Do not add a chat that becomes the sale.

Design control13

Reputation

People grade the handoff, not the personality.

After the exchange, each side gives a thumbs up or a thumbs down, and may add a private comment and a photo. A thumbs-down can be challenged. If the challenge holds, it comes off. There is a score, Rummlee Rep. Accurate descriptions and showing up add to it. Fake reports and sloppy listings take away. Neighbors can also suggest a missing fact on a listing. The seller accepts or rejects it. That is a small correction, not a comments section.

Explicit photos and explicit wording are blocked before a listing goes up. Repeat that, or list something prohibited, and the account is flagged, then suspended. Closing an account for good is a staff action. One identity, one account. A new login does not wash the score. Support can restore access. Support does not restore a blank reputation.

ID Verified is a badge. It means the name on the account matched an ID. It is free with Plus, or $4.99 once, and it is off during the test. We do not keep a picture of the ID in the product as designed. Do not promise the badge until Matthew turns the check on.

Trust14

When something goes wrong

The store holds a box. The store is not the insurer. We still have to say what happens.

Already in the product

  • Seller never drops off within 2 days: buyer refunded.
  • Box sits 5 days after the store scans it in: buyer refunded.
  • Associate refuses the package: buyer refunded. No names on the screen.
  • Seller can mark an item sold outside the app. That does not move money, because Rummlee was never paid.

Not a fee, and not a promise

  • There is no 50% charge for a buyer who walks away. Do not describe one.
  • A seller may pick an item back up, or leave it. Left items can be resold by Rummlee. Half of what Rummlee receives on that resale is for charity. The charity does not have to be in Fargo.
  • Fargo is first only because we can store a box there.
