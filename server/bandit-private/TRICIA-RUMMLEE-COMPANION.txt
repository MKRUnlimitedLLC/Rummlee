THE PRODUCT
================================================================================

Tagline, and the funnel: The good stuff, before Saturday.
Site: rummlee.com. Company in the books: Rummlee. Entity discussed internally as Rummlee Corp, a North Dakota C-corporation. Do not put owner names on the site.

What it is: a local pre-sale. The buyer pays before anyone drives. Money is held until both people confirm the handoff. Beta uses test credits. Real cards are off.

Privacy is the product. People use a handle, not a legal name, on listings. A home street never appears on Browse, a listing, or a sale card. A meetup note appears only after payment, and only for in-person handoff or for a sale that allows pickup at the event.

Words: Neighborhood sale. Handoff. Official store handoff. Public place handoff. In-person handoff.
Never: porch, driveway, meet at my house, garage-sale app.
Nothing ships.

Rank: official store first, public place as backup, in person only if the seller turns it on.
Marketed hardest to women in cities and suburbs. The marketplace is for everyone. Do not write copy that says only women may use it.

A sale:
- Seller sets dates, up to 14 days.
- Online window, default Tuesday through Thursday. Offers and pay-asking stay open. Online does not turn off during a physical sale.
- Optional live in-person hours. Public card shows days and hours and a rough distance, not a street.
- Optional note that a paid item can be picked up at the sale. Address still hidden until pay.
- Seller sets asking price (public) and lowest price (hidden).
- Buyer pays asking, or one offer. Each side gets one counteroffer. A decline ends it.
- Listing is free. Browse is free. Minimum asking is $5. That is a floor, not a fee.

If an item sells online during a physical sale: it is held so nobody else can buy it. The seller pulls it off the table, sets it aside, then taps that they did so. Only then does the card say sold online. “Sold outside” ends a listing when no Rummlee payment happened. There is no cash checkout in the app.

Handoff:
- Seller account code on the phone, hidden until tapped, hides again after about 20 seconds. A photo of it is the same as handing over the phone.
- Short code written on the box. That code is not in the buyer’s app.
- Counter scans the seller, matches the box code, shelves it.
- Buyer has a different account code for the same sale. Counter scans it and sees which box codes to pull. Buyer types the code on the box. Each box is confirmed on its own. Several items from one seller are one trip and many boxes.
- Seller has 2 days to drop off. Buyer has 5 days to pick up. Then the buyer is refunded.
- Seller is not paid for about 48 hours after both confirm.
- Camera reads a code and does not save the picture. The counter never shows a name, a price, or an address.
- Over 50 lb, not in an outer box, or needs a truck: in person only. Not a store. A couch is the example.
- “I’m on my way” is a button, not a live map of someone’s house.
- Store, buyer, and seller can rate the handoff. Thumbs only, private comment, a down can be challenged.
- The store is not paid a location fee. That slot is $0. Foot traffic is the current bet. A per-handoff store payment can be switched on later. Do not promise a store a dollar.
- Refuse at the counter refunds the buyer. No names on that screen.

Left-behind items: a seller who does not want a rejected or abandoned item back can leave it. It can become Rummlee’s to resell. Half of what Rummlee receives on that resale is charity payable. The charity does not have to be in Fargo. It can be national. There is NO 50% buyer abandonment fee. That idea is not in the product. Do not describe it.

Trust and enforcement:
- Explicit photos and explicit wording are blocked before a listing goes up.
- Repeated violations and prohibited items: flagged, then suspended, then closed.
- One ID, one account. A new account does not reset ratings.
- ID Verified is a badge. $4.99 once, or free with Plus. Stripe Identity, name must match. Rummlee does not keep a photo of the ID. Off during beta. Capped at 50 checks until Matthew says otherwise.
- Researchers apply, pick categories, one active item, 15 minutes then a hold request. They may ask the seller for another photo. Pay is $4.60 if the seller accepts ($18.40/hour for 15 minutes, highest 2026 U.S. minimum wage). Bonus $2.50 if the item sells in the top half of their range. Bonus $2.50 if it sells at full asking. Seller pays $7.99 to ask. +++ includes 5 asks a month. More can be bought one at a time.

+++ also: unlimited sale days, no item cap, seller fee 6%, five “reveal the lowest price” uses a month, early look at items being prepared with no price, and overtime (a get-rid-of-it price on unsold items, visible to +++ only).
Plus alerts: categories, handoffs, or upcoming in-person sales. Not a live tracker.

Photo fill ($0.99, suggests title and category, does not set price) is off in beta.

- $120: seller $14.40, buyer $6.00, Rummlee $20.40, seller receives $105.60.

Planning blend, a model not a result: 70% Standard sellers, 20% Plus, 10% +++, 40% of buyers on a plan, $45 item, about $6.17 to Rummlee, roughly 14% of the item, before membership dues and before card fees, support, insurance, and any future store share.

Plus pays for itself for a buyer after about $200 of purchases in a month, because 5% of $200 is $10.
