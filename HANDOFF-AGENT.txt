Name: Rummlee
Domain: rummlee.com
Tagline: The good stuff, before Saturday.
One-liner: Neighborhood finds for women in the city and the suburbs. Offer this week. Meet at a partner store, never a home address.

What it is: a national neighborhood-sale marketplace. Pre-sale offers before Saturday. In-app escrow. Pickup at an official partner store (or, second, a public place; or, optional, person to person). A handle, not a name. Nothing ships.

What it is not: a Fargo-only app, a vintage rummage brand, a driveway garage-sale aesthetic, a shipping marketplace, a social network, or a GPS tracker.

FUNNEL (priority order, not equal features)
  1. THE FINDS — furniture, kitchen, closet, kids, beauty, plants, outdoor. Offers this week while she is still editing the closet.
  2. PRIVACY — a handle, not a name. Email, legal name, and home address never go on a listing. “Privacy is the product.” Neighborhood is chosen, not tracked.
  3. HANDOFF — official partner store FIRST (locker or pickup desk, store hours, lit lot). Public place (park / library / civic lot) is BACKUP. Person to person is OPTIONAL and still posts no home address.

Haul: Fits in a bag / One-person carry / Two-person job / Needs a truck.
