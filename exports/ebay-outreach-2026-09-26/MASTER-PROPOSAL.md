## What this is, and what it is not

eBay already has local pickup. The buyer pays on eBay, receives a QR code and a 6-digit code, and the seller scans that code. The two of them then message each other and meet, usually at the seller’s address. Funds move faster once the code is scanned. The item is still a porch meetup.

That meetup is the hole. It is where furniture, bikes, instruments, and anything annoying to ship leak to Facebook Marketplace. It is also where “item not received” is a story instead of a scan.

Rummlee is not a second eBay, and it is not Close5. Close5 was another feed. Another feed splits your buyers. We are not asking you to list anything on our site.

We are the counter. A seller drops a boxed item at a partner store. The store scans a code. The buyer picks up with a different code. Neither person sees the other’s name. The scan is the proof of handoff. The handoff method is patent pending. You already trust that kind of proof. You do not have the store, and you should not hire a general manager in Fargo to get one.

Over 50 pounds, and anything that is not in a box, stays a private pickup. A couch does not go to the counter. Authenticity Guarantee does not go through us. Those items already have a door.

---

## The pilot

One metro. Twelve months. eBay listings only, on top of the local pickup you already offer.

A buyer who is near a live store, on an eligible listing, can choose **local handoff** instead of a porch meetup or a label. Eligible means: the item fits the store rule, it is not Authenticity Guarantee, and the seller opts in. The buyer pays eBay, not us. You keep the listing, the payment, the feedback, and the final value fee. We issue the codes, the store holds the box, and the scan marks the order picked up on eBay the way your code does today.

We charge a handoff fee the way you already charge a flat fee to route sneakers through authentication. The buyer pays it instead of postage. We do not take a cut of the item.

The comparison, same quarter, same metro, items over $50 that fit in a box:

- Share of orders that actually change hands
- Share of orders that become “item not received”
- Days from payment to scan

We will put your porch-pickup orders next to store orders. If the store is not better, the exclusive ends. You keep the shares either way. We are not selling a refund.

Target, not a promise of demand we do not have: one store live within 90 days of close, and 500 completed store handoffs inside twelve months. The decision metric is the comparison above, not a national user count.

## Why this price

There is no revenue multiple. Card charges are off. The live product takes test credits so the handoff can be rehearsed. Anyone who sends you a GMV chart from this stage is wasting your time. We will not.

$15 million post-money is the price of three things you would otherwise build or lose.

1. A handoff system that is already written: two codes, a counter scan, a 24-hour drop and a 24-hour pickup, escrow that releases only when both sides confirm, and a ledger that can sit beside yours.
2. A city motion. eBay will not recruit a counter in Fargo. We will.
3. Twenty-four months in which OfferUp, Facebook, and Mercari cannot hire that same counter.

The web product is live at rummlee.com. The iOS app is in App Store Connect. Android is in progress. None of that is a marketplace at scale. It is proof the counter works before a stranger’s money is in it.

A financial investor should not pay this. You are not a financial investor. You are buying an option on the part of local pickup your own help page still leaves in Messages.

## Terms we would sign

Percentages are of the fully diluted post-money capitalization. Exact shares wait for a cap table. Counsel attaches it before close.

- **Security.** Series Seed Preferred. 30 percent as-converted to eBay. 10 percent option pool, topped up before your money so hiring does not dilute you. The remainder stays with the founders and any existing holders.
- **Liquidation.** 1x non-participating. No dividend.
- **Anti-dilution.** Broad-based weighted average. Not a full ratchet.
- **Board.** Three directors. Two founder seats. One eBay seat. You are not buying control.
- **Protective provisions.** Sale of the company, a senior security, debt above $250,000, and any change to the exclusive below. Nothing else.
- **Pro rata.** On future issuances.
- **Information.** Monthly pilot dashboard. Quarterly financials. Annual budget.
- **Exclusive, 24 months from close.** In the pilot metro, we will not provide this handoff to Facebook, OfferUp, Mercari, or a similar marketplace. Our own consumer app in that metro stays up. It is how the city lead keeps the store sharp. It does not take your listing.
- **Your pickup stays yours.** Porch meetup on eBay is not shut off. Authenticity Guarantee is not routed to a store.
- **End of the exclusive.** If, at twelve months, store handoff has not beaten porch pickup on completion and on “item not received,” the exclusive lapses. The shares do not.
- **Next round.** For 24 months you may lead the next financing, on terms no worse than those offered to any other investor. You have no obligation to buy more, and no right to force a sale.

We will not invent a call option that lets you buy the rest at a fixed bargain. If the pilot works, we should negotiate that from the numbers, not from a guess made today.
